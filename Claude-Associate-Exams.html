How to use these papers

1Closed book. The lessons file stays closed. The real exam has no lessons.
2One sitting per paper. 120 minutes; the timer starts when you click your first answer and submits for you at zero.
3Review every miss before the next paper — read the explanation, then re-read the lesson it points to. Do not take two papers on the same day.
4Target: 48/60 on two consecutive papers, with no domain bar in red. If one domain stays red across papers, that unit needs another full pass.
5Multiple-response items (five per paper) say how many to select and score all-or-nothing. Count your ticks.
How to read an item. Read the last sentence first — the actual question. Then read the scenario hunting for the one decisive detail: a regulation, a decision about a person, "customer-facing", "nobody is waiting", "confidential", "every time", "the information is already supplied". Then eliminate: the option that upgrades the model for an information problem; the option that asks Claude to verify itself; the option that removes a human check; the heavier surface than the need warrants. What remains is usually one option, occasionally two — and then the tiebreaker is the one that keeps a named human accountable.

After the papers

Booking, and the day itself

1Register through the Anthropic Partner Academy (you need a Claude Partner Network organisation and its email domain), then book with Pearson VUE. Allow a week for registration. Plan any reschedule at least 48 hours ahead: the guide says 24, but Pearson VUE's Anthropic page says 48 for test centres.
2If you have not done them, the Partner Academy's Associate prep path and Anthropic Academy's free AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations and Claude 101. Work through the exam guide's three sample questions too; they are the only official items.
3The week you sit, re-check product facts on Anthropic's site: model names, memory behaviour, any new surface. The judgement principles will not have changed; product names might.
4On the day: two minutes per item; flag and move on; count ticks on multiple-response items; answer everything — there is no negative marking.
5If it does not go your way: retakes are allowed after 14, then 30, then 90 days, with at most four attempts in 12 months. The score report gives percent correct by domain; map it to the units in the lessons file.
Find the decisive detail. Prefer the human-accountable answer. Prefer the simplest sufficient solution. Answer everything.
